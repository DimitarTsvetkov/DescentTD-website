# Privacy Policy — DescentTD: Nine Circles

*Last updated: 21 November 2026*

This policy covers the game **DescentTD: Nine Circles** ("the game") for iOS and iPadOS (and Android, when released), and its website **descenttd.com**, made by **Dimitar Tsvetkov** ("we", "us").

## The short version

- There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game never asks for your name, email or any other personal details.
- Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
- If you agree, the game sends **anonymous play statistics** through Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which levels and modes are played and make the game better. If you say no, nothing is sent.
- This version of the game shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

## What the game stores on your device

The game keeps **one save file** (plus a backup copy) in its own private storage on your device. It holds:
- your progress: levels cleared, stars, Brimstone Seals, Abyss records and Weekly challenge results;
- your Virtue choices, Codex discoveries, laurels and tower skins;
- your settings: volume and music, haptics, text size, screen shake, language, and your answer to the statistics question;
- local play totals, such as souls sealed and time played;
- the app version you first played and when you first opened the game.

This file is not sent anywhere by the game, and it contains nothing that identifies you.

Your device may include the game's storage in your own backups (for example iCloud Backup), depending on your system settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

**To delete it:** use *Settings › Reset progress* in the game, or uninstall the game.

## Anonymous play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

**Nothing is sent until you say yes.** The first time you open the game, it asks: *"Help improve the game?"* Closing the question counts as "No". You can change your answer at any time in *Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous statistics*.

If you say yes, the game sends:
- **Game events:** a level started or finished (which level, mode and difficulty, win or loss, stars, waves cleared and time taken), which menu screens are opened, and whether the ending was seen.
- **Progress summary:** the deepest circle reached, total stars, chosen difficulty, the app version you started with, and which unlockable content you own.
- **Information collected by Firebase itself:** a random app-instance ID that Firebase creates on your device, plus device model, operating system version, app version, language, and session information (such as when the game was opened and for how long). Google also works out an approximate region (country or city) from your connection; it does not give us your IP address or precise location.

What we do **not** do:
- We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA on Apple devices, Advertising ID on Android) or the iOS identifier for vendors (IDFV), and we do not ask to track you.
- All of Firebase's advertising features are switched off: ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always denied.
- The statistics are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising.

We use the statistics only to understand how the game is played and to improve it, for example to find levels that are too hard. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Statistics are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

If you turn statistics off, the game stops sending them straight away. Uninstalling the game resets the app-instance ID, so a reinstall cannot be connected to earlier statistics.

## What the game does not do

- It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location services or other apps.
- It has no chat, no user profiles and no content shared with other players.
- It needs no internet connection to play. The only data it sends is the optional statistics above.

## Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases at present. If they are added, payments will be handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play: we never see your payment details. The store only tells the game that a purchase was made, so it can unlock the content.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The optional statistics are anonymous and contain no personal details.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because the game has no accounts and the statistics are anonymous, we cannot connect any statistics to you. You can always:
- withdraw consent in *Settings › Privacy*;
- delete everything stored on your device with *Settings › Reset progress* or by uninstalling the game;
- contact us with any question or request (see below).

## This website

descenttd.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other companies' servers: its fonts and images come from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Links to the App Store or Google Play take you to those stores, which have their own privacy policies.

## Changes to this policy
