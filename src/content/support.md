# DescentTD: Nine Circles Support Center

🧭 Mission

> Our mission is to make a tower defense game that is genuinely fun to think about and genuinely funny to watch: a game where every circle feels new, every soul has a personality, and losing a level makes you laugh before it makes you try again.

🔍 Vision

> Our vision is games that respect your time and your attention: games you can play anywhere, without an account, without a connection, and without having to share anything you'd rather keep to yourself.

⚖️ Values

**Every circle is a new game**
Nine circles, 91 levels. Each circle brings new scenery, new music, new souls, a new hazard and a new boss. Just when you've worked one out, down you go.

**Comedy, never cruelty**
Hell here is theatrical and silly. Souls go *poof* in a puff of smoke. There's no blood and no gore, and the jokes are about a very nervous poet, not about real people or real religions.

**Earned through play**
Every tower, upgrade, Virtue and tower skin is earned by playing. Brimstone Seals and laurels show what you've achieved; they never make the game easier.

**Privacy by design**
No account, no sign-in, no personal details. Your progress lives on your device. Anonymous play statistics are sent only if you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time.

**Plays anywhere**
DescentTD needs no internet connection. Play it on a plane, in a tunnel or anywhere else.

🆙 Continuous improvement
We listen to our players and ship improvements regularly. If a level feels unfair or something isn't working, tell us and we'll look at it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I play?**
Souls walk along the paths toward the exit. Tap an empty slot to build a tower and tap a tower to upgrade it, sell it or change its target. Every soul that slips past costs you Seal Integrity. Clear every wave to win the level. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and use the speed buttons to play at up to 3×.

**A level is too hard. What can I do?**
Try a different difficulty in Settings: Guided Tour is the gentlest, Pilgrim's Path is the standard and Abandon All Hope is for the stubborn. You also earn Grace for your stars: spend it on the seven Virtues between levels. You can reset your Virtues and rebuild them whenever you like. Virgil's Guidance (tap his portrait, then tap the map) slows every soul and reveals hidden ones when you need a moment to breathe.

**What are Brimstone Trials and Brimstone Seals?**
Beat a level with three stars and its Brimstone Trial opens: tougher, faster souls and no Virtues. Clearing it earns that level's Brimstone Seal. Collect five Seals in a circle to unlock that circle's skins for all seven towers.

**What are the Abyss and the Weekly challenge?**
Beat a circle's boss and its Abyss opens: endless waves on that circle's own map, getting deeper and meaner every wave. Your deepest wave is your record. Every Monday, the Weekly challenge brings two new rule twists, such as Tight Budget or Everything Is Drenched, to play on any level you've unlocked. Both work offline.

**Do I need an internet connection or an account?**
No. The whole game plays offline, and there's no account or sign-in.

**Is my data shared with anyone?**
Your progress and settings stay on your device. The first time you open the game, it asks whether you'd like to share anonymous play statistics: which levels are played, won or lost, and which screens are opened. We use them only to find levels that are too hard or too easy. Nothing is sent unless you say yes, and you can change your answer in *Settings › Privacy*. We never collect your name, email or contacts, and we don't track you across other apps. Full detail is in our privacy policy.

**Will I lose my progress when the game updates?**
No. Updates from the App Store leave your progress untouched. Uninstalling the game is different: that permanently deletes your save.

**Can I move my progress to a new device?**
Your progress is saved on your device, not on our servers, because we don't run any. If your device's backup includes app data (for example iCloud Backup), restoring that backup onto a new device brings your progress with it.

**How do I start again from the beginning?**
Use *Settings › Reset progress*. Tap it twice: the second tap confirms. This permanently erases all your progress, including levels, stars, Seals, records and the Codex.

**Which languages are available?**
DescentTD is currently available in English. More languages may follow.

**Is DescentTD based on Dante's poem?**
It's inspired by the fourteenth-century poem by Dante Alighieri. All the art, music, characters and jokes are original and made for this game. It isn't affiliated with any other game or film.
